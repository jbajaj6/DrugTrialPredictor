## First-public-version training experiment (2026-11-24)

Fixed scientific rule: one target NCT receives protocol features from its **first publicly posted** version only, evaluated no earlier than `max(recorded start, first-public-post date)`. Later target-trial edits are never substituted. A later-phase NCT gets its own first-public protocol plus separately dated earlier-trial results public by that checkpoint, if clinically linked and supported.
